## Overview

**Meruby's Wish** is a high-tempo 3D action combat prototype built with **Godot 4** and **Jolt Physics**. The project emphasizes responsive third-person melee and ranged combat, tight animation canceling, modular progression mechanics, and distinct stylized toon visuals.

---

## Combat & Multi-Weapon Pipeline

Combat is built on a clean **3-layer pipeline** designed for punchy feedback and clear build identity:

* **Innate Weapon Passives & Tier Scaling**: Four distinct weapons across emotional affinities—Verdant Katana (*Happy* / sustain & speed), Twin Pistols (*Rage* / raw damage & crit), Shock Gauntlets (*Romantic* / weakness & chain lightning), and Glacial Scythe (*Sad* / crowd-control slow). Weapons scale across Pod Tiers (T1–T3) with shifting damage multipliers and execution thresholds.
* **Modular Attack Definitions**: Combos are authored via modular Godot resources defining 3D hitbox shapes, windup/active/recovery timings, dash-cancel windows, directional knockback, and aerial juggle mechanics.
* **Weapon-Isolated Perfect Dodge Surges**: Timing a precise dodge triggers a brief 2.5-second burst effect that strictly matches the currently held weapon (e.g., $+50\%$ counter damage on Pistols, guaranteed shock arcs on Gauntlets, or swift heals on Katana).
* **Skills & Ultimate Climax**: A shared ability system featuring standard 10-second cooldown skills alongside a 100% charged Climax state for devastating area-of-effect ultimates.

---

## Progression Web

Character growth centers around a radial **DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph) Progression Tree**:

* **Weapon Affinity Branches**: Players spend collected essence to unlock specialized nodes across each of the 4 weapons, boosting weapon damage, critical rate/damage, elemental status potencies, and tier odds.
* **General Vitality & Defense**: Central survival upgrades including max health scaling, Second Chance revive mechanics, and essence fortune bonuses.
* **Strict Buff Isolation**: Weapon bonuses and status effects remain strictly tied to their respective weapon identity to prevent unintended stat cross-contamination.

---

## Stylized Environment Shading & Art Direction

The visual aesthetic blends anime cel-shading with expressive manga ink techniques using custom Godot spatial shaders (`environment_toon.gdshader`):

* **Manga Ink Hatching & Outlines**: Procedural cross-hatching responding to dynamic light thresholds, paired with depth/normal crease edge detection for crisp illustrated outlines.
* **Architectural & Material Roles**: Dedicated shader palettes for buildings, facades, structural framing, roofs, and modular floor tiles with localized ambient occlusion and surface weathering.
* **Foliage & Wind Dynamics**: Vertex-animated grass and trees with stylized wind sways integrated directly into the toon lighting model.

---

## Enemy AI & Encounter Systems

Enemies provide structured, readable combat rather than chaotic group swarming:

* **Token-Based Coordination**: Shared melee and ranged attack tokens prevent unfair concurrent hits, maintaining clear cadence during multi-enemy waves while allowing bosses to bypass limits.
* **Telegraphed Hazard Patterns**: Directional strikes with hyper-armor cues, floor telegraph projections, falling AoE hazards, and procedural projectile formations (fans, spirals, and rings).
* **Dynamic Decision Logic**: Tactical evaluation based on distance, line of sight, group cooldowns, and behavioral state machines.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Engine**: Godot 4.x (Forward+ Mobile/Desktop Renderer)
* **Physics**: Godot Jolt (3D Physics)
* **Shaders & Pipeline**: Custom spatial toon & manga shaders, VRM avatar pipeline, MToon integration
