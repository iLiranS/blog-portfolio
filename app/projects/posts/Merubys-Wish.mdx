## Overview

**Meruby's Wish** is a 3D action combat prototype built with **Godot 4** and **Jolt Physics**. The project focuses on responsive third-person melee mechanics, modular attack resource pipelines, and clean encounter orchestration.

---

## Combat Systems

The combat framework is structured around modular, data-driven attack resources and dynamic gauge management:

* **Data-Driven Attack Definitions**: Combos are authored via reusable `AttackDefinition` resources defining spatial collision shapes, windup/active/recovery timings, early cancel windows for dodging, knockback vectors, and aerial juggle physics.
* **4-Emotion Gauge & Skill Casts**: Real-time cycling between 4 distinct emotion meters (Rage, Happy, Romantic, Sad). Reaching full charge unlocks active casts with unique weapon trail VFX palettes and properties.
* **Keystone Empowerments**: Progression unlocks Tier 2 empowered skill variants, such as cataclysmic meteor smashes, high-gravity singularity pulls, flurry dashes, and persistent glacial freeze zones.

---

## Enemy AI & Encounter Systems

Enemies are built to provide structured, readable combat rather than chaotic group swarming:

* **Token-Based Attack Coordination**: Enemies claim shared `melee` and `range` attack tokens before striking, preventing unfair simultaneous hits while allowing bosses to dynamically bypass token constraints.
* **Attack Variations & Patterns**: Support for directional melee strikes with hyper armor, telegraph indicators for floor beams, cascading AoE drops, and procedural projectile patterns (single shots, fan spreads, rings, and spirals).
* **State & Condition Logic**: Attack selection evaluates distance, line of sight, group cooldowns, health thresholds, and selection weights.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Engine**: Godot 4.x (Forward+ Renderer)
* **Physics**: Godot Jolt (3D Physics)
* **Avatars & Shaders**: VRM model pipeline, MToon shading, custom visual effect shaders
