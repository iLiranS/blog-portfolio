**Aurelia** is a native desktop MIDI piano visualizer and practice studio designed for smooth 60fps rendering, low-latency SoundFont synthesis, and distraction-free daily practice.

🔗 **[View on GitHub](https://github.com/iLiranS/Aurelia)**

---

## ✨ Overview

Most MIDI visualizers are either heavy browser apps with audio lag or dated desktop utilities. Aurelia pairs a **Rust** audio and MIDI backend with a **Three.js (React Three Fiber)** 3D stage inside **Tauri 2**, keeping hardware input, synthesis, and rendering fast and self-contained.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎹 3D Stage & Visuals
- **60fps Instanced Waterfall & 88-Key Piano**: WebGL rendering with custom shaders, selective bloom, hit-glow particles, and key depression physics.
- **Note Labels & Measure Lines**: Optional pitch labels on falling notes and keys, plus instanced measure lines parsed directly from MIDI time signatures.
- **Customizable Themes**: Personalize stage colors, hand palettes, and visual effects from the in-app Appearance & Studio popover.

### 🔊 Native Rust Audio & MIDI
- **Low-Latency SoundFont Synthesis**: Powered by `cpal` and `rustysynth` with studio reverb, touch presets, and the bundled `SalamanderGrandPiano-V3-Lite.sf2` (plus custom `.sf2` import).
- **Plug-and-Play Hardware MIDI**: Automatic USB MIDI detection via `midir` with direct Rust-to-Rust audio routing, sustain pedal (`CC64`) support, and range dimming for `25`–`88` key controllers.
- **Offline Audio Export**: Render MIDI pieces or live recordings directly to **WAV** (`hound`) or **MP3** (`mp3lame-encoder`).

### 🎯 Study Mode & Practice Tools
- **Wait-for-Input Mode**: Pauses the waterfall at the hit line until you play the expected note(s), with chord grouping, early-hit forgiveness, and auto-skipping for out-of-range notes on compact keyboards.
- **Hand Separation & Looping**: Practice Left, Right, or Both Hands with per-track hand assignment, configurable pitch split, `0.25x–2.0x` tempo scaling, non-destructive transposition, **A–B loops**, and count-in.

### 🎙️ Free Play, Recording & Library
- **Free Play & Recording Studio**: Open the stage without a MIDI file with a built-in metronome, capture live performances, and save or export recordings.
- **Folder-Watched MIDI Library**: Persistent SQLite-backed library (`rusqlite`) with `blake3` indexing, hover audio previews, drag-and-drop importing, and customizable shortcuts (`⌘/`).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Desktop Shell & Backend**: Tauri 2, Rust (`cpal`, `rustysynth`, `midir`, `midly`, `rusqlite`, `blake3`, `hound`, `mp3lame-encoder`)
- **Frontend & 3D**: React 19, TypeScript, Three.js / React Three Fiber (`@react-three/fiber`, `@react-three/drei`), Zustand, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI
