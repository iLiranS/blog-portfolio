> **Note:** An ongoing sandbox for custom shaders, rendering techniques, and engine architecture in Godot 4.

## Overview

A technical playground built in **Godot 4** focused on stylized 3D graphics, procedural environment shaders, and diorama level architecture.

---

## Art Direction & Shaders

* **Watercolor Toon Pipeline**: Warm cream paper bases with three-tone toon shading, cool lavender-slate shadow glazes, and capillary edge darkening simulating pigment pooling.
* **Tilt-Shift & Camera Rig**: Low-FOV telephoto camera rig featuring fast box-blur depth-of-field for a miniature diorama aesthetic.
* **Painterly Foliage & Atmosphere**: Layered ribbon grass clumps with root-to-tip wind sway, translucent gradient water, and GPU-accelerated ambient motes drifting with global wind vectors.
* **Deckle-Edge Perimeter**: Procedural edge wash that dissolves room boundaries directly into paper voids.

---

## Engine & Performance

* **Spatial Chunking**: $8\times8$ grid chunking ($16\text{m} \times 16\text{m}$) enabling automatic frustum culling of $85\text{--}95\%$ of offscreen geometry.
* **Physics & Collider Batching**: Modular static colliders grouped per chunk with decoupled overhead camera occluders.
* **VRM / MToon Workflow**: Custom character shading adapted to environment lighting and shadow palettes.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Engine**: Godot 4.x (Forward+ / Forward Mobile)
* **Physics**: Godot Jolt
* **Shaders**: Custom spatial toon & watercolor shaders, GPU `MultiMeshInstance3D` particle batching
