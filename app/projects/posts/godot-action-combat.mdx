## Overview

**Meruby's Wish** is a high-tempo 3D action combat prototype built with **Godot 4** and **Jolt Physics**. The project focuses on responsive third-person combat, animation canceling, a unified defensive discharge system, stylized cel/manga visuals, and structured boss encounters.

---

## Combat & Five-Weapon System

Combat centers on responsive flow, directional knockback, and distinct weapon identities paired with a universal defensive tool:

* **Global RMB Danger Discharge**: Right-click functions as a unified emergency reaction across all weapons—releasing an air-refractive supersonic shockwave that knocks back surrounding enemies while granting a 2-hit heraldic shield barrier.
* **Five Weapon Archetypes**:
  * **Verdant Katana (Green)**: High-speed melee combos, aerial juggles, and dash-cancel mobility.
  * **Gravity Manipulation (Violet)**: Area control via persistent spatial orbs that form damaging polygonal cycles and launch into homing projectile barrages during Climax.
  * **Ink Painting Brush (Black/Ink)**: Battlefield zoning with lingering ink floor stains tracked via pooled `MultiMeshInstance3D` batching, culminating in detonating ink blooms.
  * **Glacial Staff (Blue)**: Ranged crowd control and stacking Chill that builds into full ice stasis and shattering blooms.
  * **Sniper Rifle (Red)**: Long-range piercing lane rounds, floor knockdown traps, and focused single-target execution with Dead Eye.
* **Perfect Dodge Counters**: Precise timing triggers weapon-tailored counter opportunities and rapid offensive turnarounds.

---

## World Design, Presets & Macro Loop

The world architecture links modular room environments, quest states, and encounter zoning under shared primitives:

* **Unified Room Encounter Modes**: Seamless transition between roaming exploration, peaceful hubs, lockdown combat arenas, and surreal labyrinth trials through a modular room connection graph.
* **Environment Presets & Architecture**: Authored environment profiles (`forest`, `ruins`, `village`, `labyrinth`) paired with custom Blender tooling for vertex-painted material palettes (facades, trims, window glass, slabs, and plinths) featuring ambient cavity occlusion and weather grime.
* **Declarative Quest State Machine**: Decoupled quest and stage progression engines driving world flags, stage objectives, and room re-entry priority waterfalls without hardcoding scene logic.
* **Tactical Squad Spawning**: Authors deterministic enemy groups with frontline, flank, and backline formation offsets mapped to shared combat token pools.

---

## Shaders & Art Direction

Visuals blend anime cel-shading with dynamic manga ink techniques:

* **Manga Ink Hatching & Outlines**: Procedural cross-hatching keyed to dynamic light thresholds, complemented by depth and normal-crease detection for crisp illustrated lines.
* **Perception Waves & Air Refraction**: Combat effects use screen-space distortion and charcoal perception waves rather than opaque glowing particles.
* **Modular Environments & Foliage**: Dedicated spatial shaders for stylized architectural surfaces, floor tiling, and vertex-animated wind dynamics.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Engine**: Godot 4.x (Forward+ Desktop / Mobile Renderer)
* **Physics**: Godot Jolt (3D Physics)
* **Pipeline & Shaders**: Custom spatial toon & manga shaders, MToon / VRM pipeline, procedural `MultiMeshInstance3D` VFX batching
